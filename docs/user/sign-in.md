# Signing in

You need an account to keep a todo list. Sign in with an email and a password, or with GitHub or Google when the app offers them.

## Create an account

1. Open **Create an account**.
2. Enter your name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Open the link we send you. It confirms your email and signs you in.

The link works for 24 hours. If it expired, open it anyway: the page lets you ask for a new one. Nothing in your inbox? Check the spam folder, or use **Send the link again**.

## Sign in

Enter the email and password you signed up with. If you have not confirmed your email yet, sign-in stops and we send you a new link.

After you sign in, the app takes you back to the page you tried to open, or to your todos.

**Continue with GitHub** and **Continue with Google** appear only when the app is set up for them.

## Forgot your password

1. Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your email. If an account exists for it, we send a link.
3. Open the link and enter a new password twice.

The link works for one hour and only once. Your old password keeps working until you save the new one.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** at the top of any page.

A sign-in does not last forever. When it ends, the app asks you to sign in again and then returns you to the page you were on.
